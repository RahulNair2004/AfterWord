# AfterWords

### Investigate. Understand. Debate. Reconsider.

**AfterWords** is an AI-powered social investigation platform built around real-world cases. Users can explore a case, investigate its evidence, connect information, understand what is known or uncertain, and participate in structured debates around the dilemmas that emerge.

Unlike traditional social platforms where discussion starts with opinions, AfterWords puts **investigation and evidence first**.

## How It Works

```text
Discover a Case
      ↓
Investigate
      ↓
Collect & Analyze Evidence
      ↓
Synthesize Facts
      ↓
Explore the Dilemma
      ↓
Debate
      ↓
Reconsider
```

## Key Features

* 🔎 **Case Investigation** — Explore cases through timelines, sources, evidence, claims, and hypotheses.
* 📚 **Evidence-First System** — Connect important information back to its underlying sources.
* 🧠 **AI Assistance** — Summarization, timeline extraction, evidence analysis, and other investigation assistance.
* ⚖️ **Structured Debate** — Build arguments, challenge them, ask questions, and attach supporting evidence.
* 🔄 **Reconsideration** — Users can update their position as they encounter new evidence or arguments.
* 👥 **Social Investigation** — Collaborate with other users to investigate and understand cases.
